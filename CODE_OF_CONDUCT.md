# Code of conduct

Relay contributors are expected to keep participation professional, constructive, and safe.

Be respectful of differing experience and viewpoints. Critique ideas and code with specific
technical evidence; do not attack people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable in project
spaces or when representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior threatens the community or project. Good-faith technical disagreement
is welcome; repeated disruption or intimidation is not.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.
